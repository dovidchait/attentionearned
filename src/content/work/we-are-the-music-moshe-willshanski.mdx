Eleven locations. One very long day.

On the way to one of the setups we got rear-ended hard enough that the V-mount batteries flew off the camera. Everyone was fine. The car was not. We kept going.

At one point in Brooklyn I needed people to dance on camera and had no one. I started pulling strangers off the street. One of them I asked, "do you think outside the box?" He said yes. So I asked him if he wanted to literally dance inside a box. He did it. Made the cut.
