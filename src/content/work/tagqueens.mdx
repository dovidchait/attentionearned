Tag of Queens started but had zero marketing plan. We leveraged an event they were hosting for the community to collect hundreds of email addresses, with a gated content offer of the full event recording. Then for the campaign we repurposed the raw video featuring Rabbi Elya Brudny, Joey Haber and Rabbi Drew.

We were able to position Tag as a relevant, needed org right out of the gate.
