When Yeshiva Day School Las Vegas came to us they were about to start a building campaign for a building they purchased and were about to start converting.

The issue was donors did not know what the campaign was for. Do you have a building? What is there now? What will be there after construction?

So we made this short video to show what will be and why it should be.

Ten days from the first call to coming up with the concept, flying to Las Vegas and editing with motion graphics.
