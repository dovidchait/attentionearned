Lakeshore Community Foundation had strong program outcomes and weak
donor numbers. The brief was clear: make people feel something.

We produced three short films, each following a single family through
the foundation's housing stability program. The series ran across email,
social, and a dedicated campaign landing page.

Year-over-year individual donations increased 34 percent in the campaign
window. The board approved a second series before the first finished
post-production.
