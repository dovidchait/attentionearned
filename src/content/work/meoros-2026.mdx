Last year I made Meoros Orlando's first campaign video. It had one job: explain why a girls' high school in Orlando has to exist. ([Last year's video](/work/meoros-orlando))

This year they came back with a $400,000 goal and a different problem.

## The Challenge

Mrs. Sara Gittelson [confirm title] put it in one line: "Let's face it, when is the last time you watched a school video that wasn't from your kid's school?"

People already know why Meoros exists. Last year's video took care of that. A second "why we exist" video would get a nod from the parents and nothing from anyone else.

This campaign needs funders, ambassadors, parents and students to actually raise. So the question changed. How do you get a random person from a random place to care about your kid's school?

## The Approach

Same school. Different goal. Very different video.

It always starts with what we need to say. This year the answer wasn't a story. It was a feeling: make fundraising fun, and make something people actually want to watch and share.

So we handed the girls the cameras and the questions. They interviewed their teachers, their parents and each other. "Why do you get up every morning to come and teach at Meoros?" "Who is your favorite student?" (No answer.)

They got paid in sugar. We kept the bloopers. We kept the ending, where nobody is sure the camera is still rolling.

Then we built the rollout around how people actually share: a blooper teaser, a short case study on last year vs this year, and WhatsApp status cuts that parents and students can post in one tap. Subtitles on everything, for everyone watching on mute.

## The Result

The campaign runs Oct 26 to Oct 27, 2026 on CauseMatch [confirm].

I'm not measuring this one by views. I'm measuring it by how many people raise, how many post it to their status, and how many get active in the campaign.

## The Lesson

A second campaign isn't a sequel. When the goal changes, the video should too. And when the job is getting people to share, make something they'd actually want to share.
