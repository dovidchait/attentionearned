Meridian Financial Group needed their annual report to do more than inform.
It needed to build confidence at a critical moment in the company's growth.

We produced a 12-minute documentary-style film that opened with the CFO
speaking directly to camera and moved through the year in three acts:
challenge, response, and momentum.

The film screened at the annual shareholder meeting and was embedded on
the investor relations page, where it became the most-visited asset
in three years.
