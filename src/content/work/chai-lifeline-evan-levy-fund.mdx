Evan Levy was a child who passed away. Every year, Chai Lifeline honors people in his memory through the Evan Levy Fund. This video was built from interviews shot in three different states.

Telling stories about sick kids is heart-wrenching. As producer, I coordinated every interview remotely, and the hardest part wasn't the logistics. It was being there emotionally for the people telling their stories while I wasn't in the room with them.
