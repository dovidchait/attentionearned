## What we text

Attention Earned runs a text-to-post service for the nonprofits and small businesses we make videos for. When you text or email us a video and caption, we post it to your social accounts and text you back:

- confirmations that your post is live, with a link for each platform
- notices if a post failed, and why
- reminders about scheduled posts and calendar items you set up with us
- replies to messages you send us

We do not send marketing or promotional texts from this number.

## How you opt in

You opt in by texting **START** or **JOIN** to +1 (866) 618-8109, by checking the optional SMS box on our [text updates page](/sms), or by saying yes when David asks you on a call. Texting us a video or a question also opts you in to replies about it. **Consent is not a condition of purchase or service.** You can use every part of our service without text messages.

## Opting out and help

Reply **STOP** at any time to stop all texts from this number. We'll send one message confirming you're unsubscribed, and nothing after that. Reply **START** to resubscribe.

Reply **HELP** for help, or reach us at [editors@attentionearned.com](mailto:editors@attentionearned.com) or 347-361-0822.
