Moshe, thanks for the push to cut this talking head reel. For B roll samples, check out the rest of my [site](https://attentionearned.com/cinematography-work-samples/).

Talking is the biggest component of most videos. I have shot close to a thousand heads here are some of them.

My go to gear for this type of job:

Ursa Mini 4.6k

Canon R8

Audio:

AT4053b Hyper cardioid boom mic (this extremely directional mic captures prefect quite audio in 95 percent of scenarios.

2x Countryman B3 lavs

Holyland wireless

Sony UTX B2 Wireless

Tascam X8 32 Bit six input recorder.

Grip:

4x C stands

5x Light stands

Various Cardilini type grip gear.

2x Amran 200xs

2x Kino Flow

2x Arri 1k Open face

Aperture P60x Panel

For 90 percent of single subject scenarios one 200x with a 5 FT octobox works. Perhaps a backlight or kicker if location requires.
