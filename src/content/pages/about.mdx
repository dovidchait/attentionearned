## We make video that earns attention

AttentionEarned was founded on a simple conviction: corporate video does
not have to be forgettable.

We work with companies and organizations that have something real to say
and need a production partner who can translate that into something
an audience will actually finish watching.

Our clients include healthcare systems, financial institutions, regional
nonprofits, and consumer brands at inflection points.

### What we believe

Good video starts with a good brief. Before a camera is switched on,
we want to understand who is watching, what you need them to feel or
decide, and what success looks like six months after delivery.

That discipline is what separates work that moves people from work that
fills a content calendar.

### Strategy plus production

Most production companies execute what they are handed. We think earlier.
We help clients understand what kind of video they actually need, for
which audience, on which channel, before a single shot is planned.

That integrated approach is what our clients come back for.

### Where we are based

We are based in the United States and travel for production globally.
