## Privacy Policy (aka: We’re Cool About Your Data)

Welcome to **Attention Earned**. We are Video marketers, yes… but not _that_ kind of marketer.

Here is the deal:

**No Creepy Tracking**
We do not stalk you across the internet, peek in your digital windows, or harvest every click you make. That is not our vibe.

**Minimal Cookies**
We use the tasty kind in real life, not the creepy digital kind. Any cookies here are just the basic boring ones that keep the site running smoothly. (we are probably not doing our jobs fully as we don't like using Pixels to stalk you).

**Your Info = Your Info**
If you share your email with us, maybe for a newsletter or booking a call, we will use it only for that purpose. No selling. No trading. No shady deals in dark internet alleys.

**Text Messages (SMS)**
If you opt in to texts from us at +1 (866) 618-8109, we use your mobile number only to send the messages you signed up for: post confirmations with live links, failure notices, and posting reminders. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. The only exception is the service providers that deliver the messages for us (like our texting provider), and they may use it only for that. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Full details are in our [SMS Terms](/sms-terms).

**Respect First**
We believe attention should be earned, not stolen. That goes for your trust and your data.

If you ever have questions about what we collect (spoiler: barely anything), or want us to delete your info, just reach out. We will happily oblige.

That is it. Short, sweet, and respectful.
