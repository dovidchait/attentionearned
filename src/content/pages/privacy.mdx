## Privacy Policy (aka: We’re Cool About Your Data)

Welcome to **AttentionEarned.com**. We are Video marketers, yes… but not _that_ kind of marketer.

Here is the deal:

**No Creepy Tracking**
We do not stalk you across the internet, peek in your digital windows, or harvest every click you make. That is not our vibe.

**Minimal Cookies**
We use the tasty kind in real life, not the creepy digital kind. Any cookies here are just the basic boring ones that keep the site running smoothly. (we are probably not doing our jobs fully as we don't like using Pixels to stalk you).

**Your Info = Your Info**
If you share your email with us, maybe for a newsletter or booking a call, we will use it only for that purpose. No selling. No trading. No shady deals in dark internet alleys.

**Respect First**
We believe attention should be earned, not stolen. That goes for your trust and your data.

If you ever have questions about what we collect (spoiler: barely anything), or want us to delete your info, just reach out. We will happily oblige.

That is it. Short, sweet, and respectful.
