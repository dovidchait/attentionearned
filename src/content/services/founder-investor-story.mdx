Investors have heard every pitch-deck buzzword already. A founder video that sounds like everyone else in the category doesn't move anyone. We push past the safe version of your story until we get to the one that's actually yours.

## What we produce

- Founder and investor-deck video
- Company origin and vision pieces
- Fundraise and campaign launch films

## How we work

Before we shoot anything, we figure out what you need investors to feel or decide, and what "the right thing said to the right people" looks like for your raise. Then we build a video that works in an actual investor meeting, not just on a highlight reel.
